How it works

The lab

  • Draw in the test area with mouse, finger or pen. Tap a library card to open it; tap a variation to switch to it. The dice over the variations makes six more; Undo goes back.
  • Built-in tools stay as they are: changing one makes your own copy.
  • Tap the pencil by the name to rename. New brushes are named adjective-produce, or now and then after a ballplayer from a famously garbled baseball game.
  • Save keeps the brush in this browser's library; the bin on a card deletes it.
  • The share code is the whole brush in one line. On the Manta: Brush Lab, Load a code, paste.

Randomize and lock

  • Style picks a family: pens (Fineliner, Brush pen, Dip pen, Marker, Pencil, Charcoal), paints (Watercolor, Ink wash, Gouache, Oils, Pastels, Dry brush), or Wild, a pen or a paint at random. Picking a paint style turns a pen into a paint, and back.
  • Randomize makes a new brush in that style, keeping what's locked. The shaded part of a slider is the style's range; drag past it any time.
  • Every setting has its own die, and a lock that keeps it through rolls and variations.
  • Speed works either way: its switch flips between thinner when fast, like a nib running dry, and thicker when fast, like the eraser.

Match a stroke

  • Photograph one stroke on plain paper, flat and evenly lit, filling much of the frame; or try a sample. Drag a box round the stroke if the automatic one is off.
  • The page takes out the lighting, finds the stroke's centre line and widths, guesses pen or paint and which end it started, and measures tapers, nib, dry gaps, ragged edges, drops, soft and hollow edges, grain and streaks. Then it draws candidates with the real brush engine along the same line and keeps the closest. No AI: classic image analysis and fitting.
  • Pressure and speed don't show in a photo: pressure is a guess from how the width swings; speed is left off. A pen is matched at the medium nib, a paint at medium size.
  • Wrong end? Flip the start and match again. Wrong kind? Pick pen or paint.

Textures

  • Texture turns a photo of paper or canvas into a tileable 256 px grain, like the built-in scans (CC0, ambientCG). Paper tooth (pens) and Granulation (paints) set how strongly it shows.
  • Your textures live in this browser and work here. The Manta can't load them yet: a share code carries only the six built-in textures, so a brush on your own texture travels as plain. They'll reach the Manta in a later version.
  • Brushes and textures are kept in this browser only; clearing its data clears them.
