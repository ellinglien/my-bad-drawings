# The owner's picks and ideas, 2026-10-19

From the brainstorm on novelty art tools, children's art supplies and Lynda Barry. The goal for the tools:
"useful but delightfully unpredictable".

## Brush ideas, prototyped in Mischief Lab 2 (`docs/archive/brush-lab/prototypes/mischief-lab-2.html`)
- Stay in the lines: wet paint treats pen lines as walls (colouring book).
- Potato stamp: "select an area of the screen to create a stamp or brush nib".
- Spirograph loops around the stroke, and "INTERNAL spirograph pattern within a brushstroke" (guilloche).
- Crayon as a medium: tooth, wax build-up, resists washes, scratch-through on a black page.
- Shifting swatches: "unpredictable swatch selection on pen touch ... shifting randomly, or from a set, or following a
  pattern".
- Quick mirror: "draw a straight line on a page and have it reflect ... several mirrors to a page to make it a
  kaleidoscope (to a limit if necessary)".

## The owner's answers on notebook rules and templates (2026-10-19)
- Timed pages: when time is up the page just locks. Warn well before the end: "maybe something blinking in the
  sidebar would be enough".
- One brush: chosen when creating the notebook. New notebook creation gets a set of extended options (template,
  rules, brush).
- Template guides print in the PDF by default; the notebook's options can leave them out.
- Toolbar: MonoPaint uses two toolbars (top and left). "for now if you need room you can create one, otherwise i think
  a careful design pass would be good, down the road." Smaller tool squares are fine; the owner wants "an elegant and
  smaller size" for the swatch squares ("that shape is too big"). Mockup: artifact FgoNCr31Qa1HYqzmNTx4q5.

## Notebook tools (2026-10-19)
- The owner: "for the notebook creation and edit options... it'd be cool to have a random selection of tools possible
  at the start ... custom for each notebook .. lockable and unlockable, but down the road maybe locked forever ? like
  the other constraints or timer , etc"
- So: each notebook can have its own toolbar, optionally a random set at creation; the set (and the other rules:
  no erasing, one brush, timed pages) can be open, locked (unlockable in the notebook's options), or later sealed
  for good.

## Brush designer for other apps (2026-10-19, for after the current queue)
- The owner: "it'd be cool to think about how the brush designer could be a tool for developing brushes for other apps...
  a unique idea that would be amazing to make cross platform (or just a big one like procreate?)".
- First thoughts: the web Brush Studio as the hub, with exporters. Procreate (.brushset: zip of plists plus shape/grain
  PNGs), Krita (.kpp, open), MyPaint (.myb JSON, open), maybe Photoshop .abr. What translates: tip shape, paper grain,
  pressure/tilt/speed responses, taper, spacing, scatter. What stays Inkwash-only: drying, Mold, gravity, Float, smudge
  physics, Print Shading.

## Lynda Barry's prompt style (researched 2026-10-19, for the daily prompt)
- Found documented: the timed self-portrait on an index card (2–3 min) at the start of class; drawing yourself as a
  monster, a superhero, an astronaut, an animal, or a fruit or vegetable (3–4 min each); the 4- or 6-minute daily diary
  (boxes for did, saw, heard, a drawing); Batman-speed drawings; the spiral; the 16-frame exercise; "Instant Book Review"
  (folded sheet, timer); "Face Jam", "Photo Booth".
- Style: short and timed (seconds to a few minutes), image-first, about you or your day, playful transformations, no
  skill needed, simple shapes (big round head, simple body), done with a timer and without erasing.
- The exact attendance-sheet wording wasn't found online; it's in Making Comics (2019) and Syllabus (2014). Write our own
  in this spirit; don't copy hers.
