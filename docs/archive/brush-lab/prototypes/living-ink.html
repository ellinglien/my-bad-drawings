How it lives
  • Grows only after lift, only within Reach of its own stroke.
  • 10 fixed ticks per wet second; vigour = (1 − t/life)1.4, so it slows as it dries.
  • Only darkens, never lightens: kinder to e-ink ghosting.
  • Drying here is the paint dial's curve, 0.75 s + 29.25 s × d², so 0.75–30 s.
  • Randomness from brush seed × stroke hash; paper fibres fixed to the page.
