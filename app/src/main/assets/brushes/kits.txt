# Tool kits: the brushes one way of working needs, put on the bar together from the Tools page (its Kits chip,
# or Load Kit under This Brush when the brush shown is in a kit). A kit fills the bar's first slots, in this order.
#
# A kit starts with "Kit: Its Name", then "Intro: one whole sentence" saying how the kit is used, in order.
# Then one line a brush, in the order it is used: the Basic tool's name exactly as on the Tools page, a colon,
# and its job in two or three words ("Sumi: Drop ink"). The bar's slot says "1 Drop ink" when it is picked.
# A last line "Eraser Dry Media: Erase pencil" also sets the eraser to that kind as the kit loads
# (Everything, Pen, Paint or Dry Media). At most eight brushes a kit. Lines starting with # are notes.
# Plain, warm words, no semicolons (docs/TEXT-STYLE.md).

Kit: Marbling
Intro: Drop ink and clear water in turn to grow rings, then comb them before they print.
Sumi: Drop ink
Clear water: Drop water
Comb: Comb rings

Kit: Sketch and Ink
Intro: Sketch in pencil, ink over it, then erase the pencil and the ink stays.
Sketch pencil: Sketch
Steady line: Ink over
Eraser Dry Media: Erase pencil

Kit: Watercolour with Masking
Intro: Mask the whites, wash over them, blot to lift paint, then rub the mask off.
Masking fluid: Mask whites
Soft wash: Wash over
Damp tissue: Blot to lift
Eraser Everything: Rub off mask

Kit: Colouring In
Intro: Draw shapes in ink, then scribble inside one and it fills to its lines.
Steady line: Draw shapes
Fill in: Fill shapes
